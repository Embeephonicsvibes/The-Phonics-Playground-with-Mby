OUR COURSES

Build strong literacy skills with confidence

Focused online courses designed around each learner's needs, pace and goals.

🔤

Phonics Lessons

Recognise and pronounce the 42 Jolly Phonics sounds, blend CVC words, identify beginning, middle and ending sounds, and build speaking and listening confidence.

📖

Reading Classes

Develop fluent, expressive reading, answer questions about texts, build vocabulary and encourage a love of independent reading.

✍️

Grammar

Understand parts of speech and sentence structure, use tenses, punctuation and capitalisation, write paragraphs and emails, and speak with better grammar.

📝

Spelling Lessons

Learn spelling patterns, digraphs and rules, use dictionaries and self-correction strategies, and build confident spelling skills.

✏️

Handwriting Lessons

Develop correct letter formation and pencil grip, write neatly and efficiently, transition from print to cursive, and improve presentation.

🧩

SEN Intervention/Therapy

Support pre-reading and reading at the learner's own pace using visual, auditory and kinaesthetic approaches, while building attention, memory, language and confidence.
